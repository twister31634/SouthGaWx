Independent, supplemental coverage.Storm Chaser Larry Morrell provides independent field observations and weather coverage to support public awareness. This coverage does not compete with or replace the National Weather Service, emergency management, broadcast meteorologists, public-safety agencies or other weather organizations. The goal is to help people see what is happening, understand local conditions and find official warning information.
Official warnings come first.Use multiple reliable information sources and follow National Weather Service warnings, local emergency officials and emergency notifications. Radar imagery and ChaseLink position data may be delayed or temporarily unavailable.
